About TFS JMUN

TFS Junior Model United Nations (TFS JMUN) is a platform where students come together to discuss global issues through diplomacy, negotiation and collaboration. Delegates represent countries, debate international agendas, draft resolutions and work together to find practical solutions to contemporary challenges.

Our Vision

To inspire future leaders by promoting critical thinking, diplomacy, global awareness and meaningful dialogue in a professional conference environment.

Our Mission

To provide every delegate with an enriching experience that strengthens public speaking, research, negotiation, teamwork and leadership while encouraging respect for diverse perspectives.

Why Participate?

  • Develop public-speaking and debating skills
  • Improve research and analytical thinking
  • Build confidence in negotiation
  • Meet students from different schools
  • Experience real-world diplomacy
